# Offer verification rules

## Identity by category
- Electronics: model suffix, generation, storage/RAM, voltage/plug, regional version, standalone vs bundle, importer and warranty.
- Clothing/shoes: exact size and color must have the shown price and stock; “from” pricing is not proof for the selected variant.
- Furniture/home: dimensions, material, finish, quantity, assembly and delivery. Visual resemblance does not establish identical manufacture.
- Beauty/grocery: volume/weight, concentration, unit count and unit price. Do not match perfume EDT with EDP or a refill with a complete product.
- Parts/accessories: compatibility, OEM/aftermarket, model year and connector. Ask if a missing detail prevents a safe fit.
- Used/refurbished/open-box: show separate from new; condition and warranty matter.
- General goods: manufacturer/model/GTIN are stronger than names. Identical images alone do not prove identical products or authenticity.

## Prices and availability
- Verify the specific product page, variant and seller. Category pages and search results can have stale or minimum prices.
- Exclude wholesale minimum-quantity offers from single-item ranking. Identify ex-VAT, Eilat-only, member-only, coupon, subscription, installment and trade-in prices.
- Show installments as total payable, never the monthly payment as product price.
- Include shipping and unavoidable fees only when evidenced. Unknown is not free. Do not claim tax-inclusive landed cost for overseas purchases without checking the applicable current rules and actual shipment details.
- Do not automatically stack coupons/benefits. Mark eligibility and exclusions.
- State in-stock only if the page explicitly indicates it for the selected variant. “Add to cart” alone may not establish stock; label unclear stock unknown.
- Keep the source currency; if converting, cite a current exchange-rate source and mark conversion approximate, excluding unknown card fees.
- Compare packs with unit price plus actual purchase total; a lower unit price can require greater expenditure.
- If page and structured data disagree, report the conflict or omit from ranking. Never silently select the lower number.

## Minimum evidence record
For each offer capture: product title, match type/reason, brand/model/variant, retailer/seller, URL, check date, item price and currency, shipping/fees known or unknown, delivered total only when all mandatory costs are known, stock evidence, condition, and source references. Retain enough evidence in working context to justify each output row.

## Honest result states
- Exact verified: matching identifiers and variant, live retailer price read.
- Similar verified: relevant attributes checked, differences explicit, live retailer price read.
- Candidate only: snippet-only, blocked page, ambiguous variant, or visual similarity not inspected; exclude from verified rankings.
- No verified match: give what was checked and the narrow missing detail; never fabricate an exact match to complete the table.
